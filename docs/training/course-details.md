# Curriculum Details

The Course Details Page outlines what students will study before enrolling.

## Structure

- **Overview Banner**: Prominently highlights the course title, instructor credentials, difficulty level, and rating. Exposes the primary "Enroll" button.
- **Accrued outcomes list**: High-contrast bullets detailing topics.
- **Syllabus Accordions**: Collapsible panels that expand to reveal lessons, durations, and completion ticks.
- **Pre-requisites & FAQs**: Side-panel detailing equipment requirements and certification information.
