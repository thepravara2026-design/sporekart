# Learner Dashboard Design

The Training Dashboard serves as the central hub for student progression.

## Key Sections

- **Analytics Bar**: Quick stats indicating hours logged, completed courses, and certificate records.
- **Resume Study Widget**: Renders details of the active in-progress course, including a visual completion progress bar and a "Resume Lecture" button.
- **Quick Action Grid**: Quick navigation grids connecting to the catalog, my learning progress, and schedule calendars.
- **AI Course Advisor**: Recommends appropriate courses based on purchase records.
