# Feedback & NPS Form

Captures customer satisfaction reviews to guide future developments.

## Elements Details

- **Hoverable stars selection**: Five rating stars that fill/color dynamically based on hover and click states.
- **NPS labels**: Translates star selections into text descriptions (e.g. Excellent, Needs Improvement).
- **Suggestions textarea**: Logs feature requests or bug reports.
- **Confirmation dialog**: Shows successful submission banners.
