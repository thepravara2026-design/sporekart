# Wishlist Design

The Wishlist workspace organizes saved items in a grid layout.

## Layout & Controls

- **Tab Navigation Bar**: Lets users toggle between "My Wishlist", "Saved for Later", and "Recently Viewed" in a single dashboard workspace.
- **Filters Row**:
  - Search bar to filter wishlist names.
  - Sort selection dropdown (Name, Price low-to-high, Price high-to-low, Rating).
  - Share Wishlist button to copy shared links to the clipboard.
- **Product Card Layout**:
  - Styled with borders, hover transitions, and rounded corners.
  - Stock labels: "IN STOCK" (green), "LOW STOCK" (amber), "OUT OF STOCK" (red).
  - Rating indicators showing average reviews.
  - Actions: "Move to Cart" button (disabled when Out of Stock) and "X" close button to remove items.
