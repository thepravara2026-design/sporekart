# User Personas — SporeKart Enterprise AI Platform
## Phase 5 / Sprint 19 (Part 1A): Enterprise Product Experience Foundation

> **Status:** Documentation Only. These personas ground every design and journey
> decision. They are reference archetypes, not real individuals. Each includes
> Goals, Pain Points, Typical Tasks, Device Usage, and Accessibility Considerations.

---

## 1. Customer (Retail Buyer)
**Name:** Meena Iyer
**Role:** Home gardener and small-scale buyer purchasing seeds, inputs, and tools.

- **Goals:** Find quality products quickly, trust what she buys, track delivery.
- **Pain Points:** Overwhelmed by options, unsure what suits her needs, fears
  counterfeit inputs.
- **Typical Tasks:** Search products, read descriptions, compare, checkout, track order.
- **Device Usage:** Primarily smartphone (Android), occasional desktop.
- **Accessibility Considerations:** Prefers larger tap targets, clear prices,
  plain-language product info; may use browser zoom.

---

## 2. Farmer
**Name:** Suresh Patil
**Role:** Smallholding farmer cultivating multiple crops across a few acres.

- **Goals:** Buy genuine seeds/inputs at fair price, get agronomy guidance,
  improve yield reliably.
- **Pain Points:** Low literacy with English UI, poor network, needs vernacular
  clarity, distrusts unknown sellers.
- **Typical Tasks:** Browse by crop, view recommendations, order, check status,
  consult AI assistant.
- **Device Usage:** Budget Android phone, mobile data, often outdoors.
- **Accessibility Considerations:** Vernacular support, high-contrast text,
  minimal data usage, large touch targets, screen-reader friendly.

---

## 3. Grower (Commercial / Greenhouse)
**Name:** Anjali Deshmukh
**Role:** Commercial greenhouse and contract grower managing larger volumes.

- **Goals:** Bulk ordering, consistent supply, data-driven crop planning.
- **Pain Points:** Needs batch traceability, reliable ETAs, integration with
  records.
- **Typical Tasks:** Bulk cart, repeat orders, track shipments, view analytics.
- **Device Usage:** Tablet in field, desktop in office.
- **Accessibility Considerations:** Dense but readable tables, keyboard
  navigation for desktop workflows.

---

## 4. Trainer (Agronomy Educator)
**Name:** Dr. Rajeev Nair
**Role:** Agricultural trainer delivering workshops and courses via SporeKart.

- **Goals:** Publish training, register attendees, track engagement.
- **Pain Points:** Manual registration, poor attendance visibility.
- **Typical Tasks:** Create training, manage registrations, view dashboard.
- **Device Usage:** Laptop primary, projector for sessions.
- **Accessibility Considerations:** Clear forms, exportable lists, readable
  presentation modes.

---

## 5. Distributor
**Name:** Kavita Menon
**Role:** Regional distributor managing inventory and fulfillment to retailers.

- **Goals:** Efficient order processing, inventory accuracy, partner visibility.
- **Pain Points:** Fragmented tools, manual reconciliation, delays.
- **Typical Tasks:** Manage orders, update stock, coordinate logistics.
- **Device Usage:** Desktop for operations, phone for alerts.
- **Accessibility Considerations:** Fast keyboard flows, clear status colors
  (not color-only), audit trails.

---

## 6. Administrator
**Name:** Arjun Shetty
**Role:** Platform administrator overseeing users, content, and operations.

- **Goals:** Maintain platform health, resolve issues, configure policies.
- **Pain Points:** Too many disconnected admin screens, slow incident response.
- **Typical Tasks:** User management, content moderation, config, monitoring.
- **Device Usage:** Desktop, multi-monitor.
- **Accessibility Considerations:** Enterprise density with AA contrast,
  full keyboard operation, clear focus management.

---

## 7. Support Executive
**Name:** Priya Rao
**Role:** Customer support handling queries and complaints.

- **Goals:** Resolve tickets fast, access user context, escalate cleanly.
- **Pain Points:** Lack of order/user context, repetitive flows.
- **Typical Tasks:** Search orders/users, respond, escalate, log resolution.
- **Device Usage:** Desktop, headset; sometimes mobile.
- **Accessibility Considerations:** Efficient search, keyboard shortcuts,
  readable conversation threads.

---

## 8. Business Owner
**Name:** Hemant Kulkarni
**Role:** Owner of an agribusiness buying and reselling through SporeKart.

- **Goals:** Margin visibility, bulk procurement, business analytics.
- **Pain Points:** No consolidated view of spend and performance.
- **Typical Tasks:** Procurement, review analytics, manage team access.
- **Device Usage:** Desktop and tablet.
- **Accessibility Considerations:** Clear dashboards, exportable reports,
  role-based views.
