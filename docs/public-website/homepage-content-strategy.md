# Homepage Content Strategy

The SporeKart homepage copy is production-ready in structure and tone. Final
numbers, imagery, and partner/certification logos remain placeholders pending
stakeholder input.

## Voice & Tone
- **Professional & trustworthy:** we are a credible agri-tech provider.
- **Farmer-friendly:** plain language, no jargon where avoidable.
- **Tech-enabled:** we use data, labs, and systems — stated as benefits, not features.

| Section | Role | CTA |
|---------|------|-----|
| Hero | Value proposition + primary action | Browse products |
| StoryBand | Brand belief & purpose | (scroll) |

| NewsletterCta | Community capture | Subscribe |

## Content Rules
- One H1 (hero). Logical H2/H3 sequence.
- No invented statistics — metrics carry a `*placeholder` marker.
- Every section guides the user to a next step (internal link).
- Headlines are benefits-led; body copy is concrete.

## Open Items (stakeholder)
- Real trust metrics (farmers served, tonnes, labs, districts).
- Real partner / certification names & logos.
- Final hero headline A/B variants.
- Legal/region-specific claims review.
