# Homepage Storytelling

Storytelling on the SporeKart homepage exists to build belief before the user
reaches a product or training page.

## StoryBand
Positioned immediately after the hero, the `StoryBand` communicates three pillars:

1. **Who we are** — SporeKart is India’s trusted mushroom cultivation ecosystem,
   connecting lab-verified spawn, fresh and dried mushrooms, training, and support.
2. **Why mushrooms matter** — nutrient-dense food, sustainable cultivation, and a
   viable income source for farmers.
3. **Why we exist** — to make cultivation predictable and profitable through
   technology, verified inputs, and farmer-first support.

## Storytelling Principles
- Lead with the customer’s world (the farmer), not the company.
- Use concrete, relatable language (“from first spore to thriving business”).
- Keep the band scannable: three short blocks, each with an icon + heading + line.
- Story eyebrows (`Our story`, `Why mushrooms`, `Why SporeKart`) repeat the same
  narrative anchors across sections for consistency.

## Where Story Appears Elsewhere
- `HeroSection`: trust badges + belief line.
- `CultivationJourney`: the “from spore to business” narrative as steps.
- `SuccessStories`: proof that the story is real (testimonials).
- `WhyChoose`: the *why we exist* translated into differentiators.
