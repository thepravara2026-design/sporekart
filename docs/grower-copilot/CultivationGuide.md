## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely Cause | Correction |
|---------|-------------|------------|
| Slow mycelial growth | Low temperature, dry substrate | Adjust temp to optimal, add moisture |
| No pinning after expected window | CO₂ too high, temp fluctuation | Increase ventilation, stabilize temp |
| Long, thin stems | Insufficient light (Oyster) | Increase to 500–1000 lux |
| Cap cracking | Low humidity | Misting, increase RH to >85% |
| Yellowing / soft spots | Bacterial infection | Remove affected, reduce humidity, increase ventilation |
| Sporulation (premature) | Temperature stress, harvest delay | Harvest earlier, stabilize environment |
| Compost overheating | Overactive composting, NH₃ | Turn compost, increase aeration |

## Water Management

- **Quality:** Clean potable water, pH 6.5–8.0, TDS <500 ppm, chlorine <5 ppm
- **Method:** Fine misting (never direct spraying on fruiting bodies)
- **Frequency:** 2–4 times daily during fruiting depending on evaporation rate
- **Quantity:** Substrate should be at field capacity; avoid waterlogging
- **Rainwater harvesting:** Recommended where available; filter and test before use

## Ventilation

- **Minimum air exchange:** 4–8 air changes per hour during fruiting
- **CO₂ target:** <1000 ppm during fruiting (measured at canopy level)
- **Fresh air intake:** Filtered to prevent spore and pest entry
- **Exhaust:** Negative pressure system; HEPA filtration recommended in high-density facilities
- **Seasonal adjustment:** Increase air exchange during monsoon (high humidity), reduce during dry summer months
- **Natural ventilation:** Ridge vents + side curtains for smallholder facilities (minimum 15% wall area opening)
