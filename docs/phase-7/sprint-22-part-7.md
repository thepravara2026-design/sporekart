# Phase 7 · Sprint 22 · Part 7
## Enterprise Customer Intelligence • Personalization • Digital Experience

This sprint completes the Phase 7 Customer Experience Platform, unifying orders, academy progress, tickets, and rewards under an intelligent personal workspace.

### Deliverables
1. **Mock Data** ([mockData.ts](file:///f:/The%20Pravara/clients/sporekart/sporekart/frontend/web-app/src/features/customer/intelligence/mockData.ts)): Growers metadata profiles, point scales, activity feeds logs, milestones catalog, and harvest outputs datasets.
2. **Personalized Home** ([PersonalizedHome.tsx](file:///f:/The%20Pravara/clients/sporekart/sporekart/frontend/web-app/src/features/customer/intelligence/PersonalizedHome.tsx)): Dynamically greeted portal displaying continuing course widgets, recent orders timeline cards, and product recommendations.
3. **Grower Insights** ([CustomerInsights.tsx](file:///f:/The%20Pravara/clients/sporekart/sporekart/frontend/web-app/src/features/customer/intelligence/CustomerInsights.tsx)): Account health dashboard with completion progress gauges, verification badges, and smart AI insights lists.
4. **Activity Logs** ([ActivityFeed.tsx](file:///f:/The%20Pravara/clients/sporekart/sporekart/frontend/web-app/src/features/customer/intelligence/ActivityFeed.tsx)): Chronological log feed supporting category filters tabs.
5. **Recommendations Hub** ([RecommendationHub.tsx](file:///f:/The%20Pravara/clients/sporekart/sporekart/frontend/web-app/src/features/customer/intelligence/RecommendationHub.tsx)): Combined recommendations sliders for cultivars, kits, and academy courses.
6. **Achievement Center** ([AchievementCenter.tsx](file:///f:/The%20Pravara/clients/sporekart/sporekart/frontend/web-app/src/features/customer/intelligence/AchievementCenter.tsx)): Earned badges display grid and loyalty point redemption controls.
7. **Progress Milestones** ([ProgressCenter.tsx](file:///f:/The%20Pravara/clients/sporekart/sporekart/frontend/web-app/src/features/customer/intelligence/ProgressCenter.tsx)): Journey checklist tracker.
8. **Grower Analytics** ([AnalyticsDashboard.tsx](file:///f:/The%20Pravara/clients/sporekart/sporekart/frontend/web-app/src/features/customer/intelligence/AnalyticsDashboard.tsx)): Monthly yields targets graphs, sterility success margins, and AI yield forecasts.
9. **Digital Assistant**: Integrates floating overlays widgets with suggested quick questions and instant answers.
10. **Wired Routing**: Registered `/dashboard/personalized` and previews inside `App.tsx`.
