# Phase 7 · Sprint 22 · Part 6
## Enterprise Customer Support & Help Center Experience

This sprint delivers a customer support workspace for SporeKart enterprise clients, detailing ticket managers, searchable knowledge base articles, interactive FAQ accordions, and rating feedback forms.

### Deliverables
1. **Mock Data** ([mockData.ts](file:///f:/The%20Pravara/clients/sporekart/sporekart/frontend/web-app/src/features/customer/support/mockData.ts)): Model definitions and initial data sets for tickets, chat timelines, KB articles, and FAQs.
2. **Help Center Dashboard** ([SupportDashboard.tsx](file:///f:/The%20Pravara/clients/sporekart/sporekart/frontend/web-app/src/features/customer/support/SupportDashboard.tsx)): Central dashboard containing open/resolved counters, quick actions, featured articles, and WhatsApp helplines.
3. **Ticket Management** ([TicketsPage.tsx](file:///f:/The%20Pravara/clients/sporekart/sporekart/frontend/web-app/src/features/customer/support/TicketsPage.tsx)): Dynamic message chat timeline with text input to send replies and status toggles.
4. **Knowledge Base** ([KnowledgeBasePage.tsx](file:///f:/The%20Pravara/clients/sporekart/sporekart/frontend/web-app/src/features/customer/support/KnowledgeBasePage.tsx)): Searchable article catalog with helpfulness rating buttons.
5. **FAQ Center** ([FaqCenterPage.tsx](file:///f:/The%20Pravara/clients/sporekart/sporekart/frontend/web-app/src/features/customer/support/FaqCenterPage.tsx)): Accordion questions list categorized by topic.
6. **Contact Form** ([ContactSupportPage.tsx](file:///f:/The%20Pravara/clients/sporekart/sporekart/frontend/web-app/src/features/customer/support/ContactSupportPage.tsx)): Form containing category, priority select dropdowns, and file dropboxes.
7. **Feedback Form** ([FeedbackPage.tsx](file:///f:/The%20Pravara/clients/sporekart/sporekart/frontend/web-app/src/features/customer/support/FeedbackPage.tsx)): Rating stars indicator, category selectors, and suggestions logs.
8. **Routing integration**: Wired child routes under `/dashboard/support` and preview pages.
