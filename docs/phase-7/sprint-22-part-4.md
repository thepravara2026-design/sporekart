# Phase 7 · Sprint 22 · Part 4
## Customer Engagement • Wishlist • Personalized Experience

This sprint delivers a fully-fledged authenticated customer engagement workspace that includes product bookmarks, saved checkouts, regional recommendations, a notification center, and points-based gamification programs.

### Deliverables
1. **Mock Data** ([mockData.ts](file:///f:/The%20Pravara/clients/sporekart/sporekart/frontend/web-app/src/features/customer/engagement/mockData.ts)): Mock models representing wishlists, saved carts, browsing logs, personalized shelves, notifications feed, achievements, and rewards.
2. **Wishlist Workspace** ([WishlistPage.tsx](file:///f:/The%20Pravara/clients/sporekart/sporekart/frontend/web-app/src/features/customer/engagement/WishlistPage.tsx)): Dynamic grid workspace rendering wishlist filters, stock status indicators, move to cart triggers, and split tabs for Saved for Later and Browsing History.
3. **Recommendations** ([RecommendationsPage.tsx](file:///f:/The%20Pravara/clients/sporekart/sporekart/frontend/web-app/src/features/customer/engagement/RecommendationsPage.tsx)): Dynamic recommended shelves paired with an interactive AI recommendation model simulator.
4. **Notification Center** ([NotificationsPage.tsx](file:///f:/The%20Pravara/clients/sporekart/sporekart/frontend/web-app/src/features/customer/engagement/NotificationsPage.tsx)): Inbox alerting users on orders, training completions, price changes, and promotions.
5. **Loyalty Hub** ([EngagementHubPage.tsx](file:///f:/The%20Pravara/clients/sporekart/sporekart/frontend/web-app/src/features/customer/engagement/EngagementHubPage.tsx)): Gamified grower achievements list, points progression tier cards, point-redemption stores, and referral links sharing.
6. **Wired Routing**: Nested route configs in `App.tsx` matching `/dashboard/wishlist`, `/dashboard/saved-items`, `/dashboard/recently-viewed`, `/dashboard/recommendations`, `/dashboard/notifications`, `/dashboard/engagement`.
7. **Scaffolded Previews** ([EngagementPreviews.tsx](file:///f:/The%20Pravara/clients/sporekart/sporekart/frontend/web-app/src/features/customer/engagement/preview/EngagementPreviews.tsx)): Verified previews for lists, recommendations, notifications, and points stores.
