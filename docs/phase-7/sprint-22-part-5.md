# Phase 7 · Sprint 22 · Part 5
## Enterprise Learning Experience & Training Platform

This sprint delivers a premium Learning Experience Platform (LXP) for SporeKart customers, helping them acquire sterile laboratory skills and maximize commercial mushroom yields.

### Deliverables
1. **Mock Data** ([mockData.ts](file:///f:/The%20Pravara/clients/sporekart/sporekart/frontend/web-app/src/features/customer/training/mockData.ts)): Mock database of intermediate & advanced courses, lessons details, verifiable certificates, and live webinar RSVP status events.
2. **Training Dashboard** ([TrainingDashboard.tsx](file:///f:/The%20Pravara/clients/sporekart/sporekart/frontend/web-app/src/features/customer/training/TrainingDashboard.tsx)): Learner homepage with progression metrics, active resume course links, quick shortcuts, and upcoming webinar lists.
3. **Course Library** ([CourseLibrary.tsx](file:///f:/The%20Pravara/clients/sporekart/sporekart/frontend/web-app/src/features/customer/training/CourseLibrary.tsx)): Multi-category catalog browser supporting level filters and duration sorting.
4. **Course Details** ([CourseDetails.tsx](file:///f:/The%20Pravara/clients/sporekart/sporekart/frontend/web-app/src/features/customer/training/CourseDetails.tsx)): Accordion curriculum syllabus grids detailing instructors, learning requirements, and enrollment toggles.
5. **Video Classroom** ([VideoLearningPage.tsx](file:///f:/The%20Pravara/clients/sporekart/sporekart/frontend/web-app/src/features/customer/training/VideoLearningPage.tsx)): Dual split-screen video player workspace detailing text transcripts, notes fields, and completed checkboxes.
6. **My Learning** ([MyLearningPage.tsx](file:///f:/The%20Pravara/clients/sporekart/sporekart/frontend/web-app/src/features/customer/training/MyLearningPage.tsx)): Study console tracking progress metrics and review triggers.
7. **Certifications** ([CertificatesPage.tsx](file:///f:/The%20Pravara/clients/sporekart/sporekart/frontend/web-app/src/features/customer/training/CertificatesPage.tsx)): Printable credentials cabinet exposing digital PDF downloads and simulated QR security scanners.
8. **Live Calendars** ([TrainingSchedulePage.tsx](file:///f:/The%20Pravara/clients/sporekart/sporekart/frontend/web-app/src/features/customer/training/TrainingSchedulePage.tsx)): Webinar rosters detailing remaining slots and registering RSVP flags.
9. **Wired Routing**: Updates to `App.tsx` nested routers and preview wrappers.
