# Customer Journey Validation

This document certifies the continuity of the core customer journey flows.

## Validated Journey: Visitor to Verified Commercial Grower

1. **Authentication**: Client logs in securely, greeted on the personalized dashboard `/dashboard/personalized`.
2. **Growth Planning**: Audits recommendations and adds Pink Oyster Spawn to wishlist.
3. **Execution & Purchasing**: Checks out. Tracks the shipment on `/dashboard/orders/:id/track`.
4. **Learning Loop**: Enrolls in Sterile Laboratory Techniques via `/dashboard/training`. Ticks checkboxes on the classroom page to complete the class and unlock a verifiable completion certificate.
5. **Support Loop**: Encounters logistics hold questions or plate contamination issues; opens a ticket at `/dashboard/support/contact`. Tracks response timelines and sends replies in real time.
6. **Retention Loop**: Earns milestones points inside `/dashboard/achievements` and redeems store vouchers.
