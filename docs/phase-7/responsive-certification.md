# Responsive Layouts Certification

Certification of responsive performance across all viewports.

## Audited Breakpoints

- **Desktop (>=1280px)**: Multi-column layouts, 3-column catalog grids, and split video panels render as intended.
- **Tablet (834px - 1023px)**: Columns collapse, and banners wrap.
- **Mobile (390px)**: Cards adapt to single column formats. Video players maintain their aspect ratios (16:9) to prevent letterboxing.
- **Interactive Targets**: Buttons and inputs are configured to be >=48x48px on mobile devices.
