# Intelligence Reports & Insights

## Reports
The Reports section provides combined visual reports across warehouse and stock domains:
- Warehouse Utilization Report — bar chart of utilization by warehouse
- Stock Level Report — bar chart of stock by status
- Stock Distribution — donut chart of stock status distribution

## Insights
The Insights section displays actionable intelligence recommendations generated from inventory data:

| ID | Type | Title | Action |
|----|------|-------|--------|
| i1 | Warning | Low Stock Alert | View Stock |
| i2 | Alert | Warehouse Near Capacity | View Warehouse |
| i3 | Warning | High Damaged Inventory | Investigate |
| i4 | Info | Large Pending Inspection | View Queue |
| i5 | Alert | High Expiry Risk | View Expiry |
| i6 | Success | Inventory Trend Positive | — |

## Alerts
The Alerts section displays system notifications with severity categorization:

| ID | Severity | Category | Title |
|----|----------|----------|-------|
| a1 | Critical | Stock | Critical Stock Out |
| a2 | High | Warehouse | Warehouse Overflow |
| a3 | High | Quality | Batch Recall Notice |
| a4 | Medium | Receiving | Inspection Delay |
| a5 | Low | Stock | Discrepancy Detected |

Alert colors: critical (red), high (yellow), medium (blue), low (gray). Dimmed display for acknowledged alerts.
