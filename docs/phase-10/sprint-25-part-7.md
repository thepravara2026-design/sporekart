# Sprint 25 Part 7 — Enterprise Purchase Receiving, Goods Receipt, Quality Inspection & Inbound Operations

## Objective
Build the complete Enterprise Purchase Receiving Platform. This sprint establishes the permanent Inbound Operations and Goods Receipt Foundation — supporting future Supplier Deliveries, Goods Receipt, Goods Acceptance, Goods Rejection, Quality Inspection, Batch Assignment, Warehouse Allocation, Procurement, Purchase Orders, and Manufacturing Receipts.

## Scope
- New module: `admin/modules/receiving/`
- Reuses certified components from Sprint 24 and Sprint 25 Parts 1–6
- Mock Mode only — all data generated by `receivingMockService.ts`
- Token CSS variables only (`--color-*`, `--radius-*`, `--text-*`, `--space-component-gap`)
- WCAG 2.2 AA compliance
- Responsive 320–1920px
- DO NOT MODIFY: Customer Website, Auth, RBAC, Supabase, DB, Sprint 24, Sprint 25 Parts 1–6, Enterprise Design System

## Domain Model
```
Supplier Placeholder
  ↓
Purchase Placeholder
  ↓
Goods Receipt ← 45 receipts, 6 suppliers, 6 products, 5 warehouses
  ↓
Inspection ← 30 inspection records (visual/quality/packaging/quantity/documentation/temperature/humidity/laboratory)
  ↓
Acceptance / Rejection ← 6 acceptance statuses · 9 rejection reasons
  ↓
Warehouse Allocation ← 20 allocation records (zone/rack/shelf/bin/storage type)
  ↓
Batch Association ← 20 batch assignments (lot code/expiry/shelf life/quality status)
  ↓
Inventory Activation (future)
```

## Key Features
- 15-section receiving workspace covering the full inbound lifecycle
- 10-status receipt lifecycle (draft → pending → receiving → inspection → approved/rejected → completed)
- 11-status inspection framework (visual, quality, packaging, quantity, documentation, temperature, humidity, laboratory, passed, failed)
- 6 acceptance statuses + 9 rejection reasons
- 4-category storage types (ambient, cold_storage, dry, hazardous)
- Warehouse allocation with full location hierarchy (zone/rack/shelf/bin)
- Batch assignment with lot code, expiry date, shelf life, and quality status
- 8-step visual receiving workflow
- Sortable, paginated, selectable ReceivingTable with bulk actions
- 9 validation rules with pass/warning/failed status
- 8 KPI analytics cards
