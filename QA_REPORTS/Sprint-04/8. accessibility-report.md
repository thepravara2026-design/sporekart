## Recommendations

1. **Focus outline fallback (Medium):** Add custom focus styles to the 2-3 components that suppress `outline: none` without providing alternatives
2. **Semantic HTML (Low):** Replace generic `<div>` containers with `<section>`, `<article>`, `<aside>` where appropriate (~15% of page areas)
3. **Complementary landmarks (Low):** Add `role="complementary"` or `<aside>` to sidebar regions consistently
4. **Hamburger menu keyboard UX (Low):** Reduce Tab steps from hamburger toggle to first menu item
