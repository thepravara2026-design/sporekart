# Risk Dashboard Operations

## Dashboard
The Risk Dashboard provides a centralized view of business risks across 10 categories with severity classification.

## Summary Cards
| Card | Description |
|---|---|
| Total Risks | Count of all assessed risks |
| Critical Risks | Count of CRITICAL severity risks |
| Avg Score | Average risk score across all risks |
| Mitigated | Count of risks with mitigation plans |

## Risk Table Columns
- Category (badge)
- Risk
- Likelihood (1–5)
- Impact (1–5)
- Score (0–100 with progress bar)
- Severity (color-coded badge)
- Mitigation

## Severity Thresholds
| Severity | Score Range |
|---|---|
| CRITICAL | 81–100 |
| HIGH | 61–80 |
| MEDIUM | 41–60 |
| LOW | 0–40 |

## Risk Categories
REVENUE, INVENTORY, OPERATIONAL, MARKETPLACE, CUSTOMER, VENDOR, TRAINING, PLATFORM, AUTOMATION, AI
